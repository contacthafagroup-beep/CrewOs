# Market, ICP, and Pricing

## Who feels the pain this week (ICP)

**Primary: founder-led B2B service businesses, 2–20 people** — marketing agencies, dev shops, consultancies, fractional executives. They:
- already buy freelancers/Upwork for prospecting, content, proposals ($500–$3,000/mo per function),
- feel the cost personally (founder is the one doing the admin work at 11pm),
- can buy without procurement (credit card, no committee),
- churn less when the system holds their pipeline and brand voice.

**Secondary: SMB operators with repetitive comms** (studios, clinics, property managers) — enter via a single agent (e.g., review responses), expand agent-by-agent.

**Geography:** US → UK/EU (English-first, high SMB SaaS spend). Ethiopia as a build-cost/sourcing advantage and a services market later — not a launch pricing market.

## Positioning

> **"Hire AI employees, not freelancers."**

Not "AI writing tool" (competes with $29/mo) — an **operating system for AI staff** (compares against $2k–$8k/mo humans). The human approval queue is the trust wedge that closes the credibility gap every "full auto" competitor has.

Three-line pitch used everywhere:
1. Your team spends 10–20 hours/week on prospecting, content, and proposals.
2. CrewOS hires AI employees that do that work daily, for the cost of one freelance day.
3. Nothing ships without your approval — you keep the judgment, they do the typing.

## Competitive landscape (qualitative)

| Segment | Who | Their weakness we exploit |
|---|---|---|
| AI SDR/lead tools | well-funded point solutions | single-purpose, expensive, "full-auto" spam reputation; we're multi-agent + approval-gated + transparent usage |
| AI content suites | writing tools | no business-system framing (pipeline, approvals, brand settings, documents); no ROI story per seat |
| Proposal software | template tools | static templates, no generation from brief; we generate + brand + track |
| Agencies/doers | humans | 10–50x our price, slower, don't scale with the customer |

Pricing consequence: anchor against **headcount**, gate by **run volume** (which maps to money saved), never by "seats only" (that caps expansion).

## The 10x ROI test (feature gate)

Every feature must plausibly save 10× its price in labor. $499/mo needs $4,980/mo saved ≈ 33 hours at $150/hr. One Outreach run (25 leads + sequences ≈ 6–10 hours of SDR work) pays for most of a month. This is the slide, the email, the demo script.

## Revenue math to be honest about

- $150k in one calendar month from $0 requires ~75 Growth-equivalent customers in ~4 weeks — that is a **sales outcome**, not a build outcome. The code removes the build excuse; the playbook (`02-gtm-30day.md`) is the only lever that moves revenue. Treat the first-month target as: ship → 10 design partners → 3–5 paying → case studies; the $100k/mo line is a 6–12 month trajectory with quarterly re-plans, not a month-1 promise. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling a course.
