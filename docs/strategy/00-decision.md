# Decision Record — why CrewOS is the wedge

> Scores below are structured judgment (the framework you specified), not market data. They exist to make the choice auditable and re-runnable — when real sales conversations contradict a score, change the score.

## The filter chain

1. Businesses already pay humans $500–$5,000/month to do the work (proven budget exists).
2. Output is **reviewable text/documents** (what LLMs are genuinely good at in 2026 — no fragile browser automation).
3. Recurring by nature (weekly work, not one-off projects).
4. Sellable by cold outreach itself — the product can demonstrate itself (an outreach platform sold via outreach is its own case study).
5. Buildable solo on free tiers in days.

## Why the AI Workforce Platform wins

1. **It contains the top ideas instead of competing with them.** Ideas #2, #3, and #10 are literally three of its agents (Proposal, Outreach, Content). One platform, three proven budgets — the wedge captures the others' best customers.
2. **Pricing power.** "An AI employee" reframes cost against a human/freelancer salary ($2,000–$8,000/mo), not against a $29 tool. That's what makes $199–$999/mo rational — the 10x-ROI test has a real anchor.
3. **The product demos itself through its own sales motion.** CrewOS's Outreach Agent is the outreach engine. Founder-led distribution isn't parallel to the product; it's powered by it.
4. **Compounding moat-ish surface.** Every run adds workspace-specific data (brand voice, lead history, approvals). Switching means re-training your crew — modest but real retention advantage.
5. **Scope control.** The launch set is 3 agents on one shared runtime — buildable to production quality solo, unlike browser-automation-heavy ideas that break constantly and generate support load.

**Honest risks (tracked, not hidden):** crowded "AI SDR" narrative → counter by selling outcomes + human approval queue, not "AI magic"; platform dependency on model providers → router abstracts them; SMB churn → annual discounts + credits + approval-queue lock-in.

## What was intentionally NOT built first

Marketplaces, native outreach email-sending (deliverability/compliance), browser automation agents, mobile apps, white-label. All are in the roadmap (`03-roadmap-90day.md`) gated behind revenue.
