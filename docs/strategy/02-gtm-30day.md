# 30-Day Sales Playbook ($0 budget, founder-led)

> Operating principle: the product is built. Every hour now goes to conversations. Daily non-negotiable: **25 outreach touches + 5 follow-ups + 1 piece of content**, tracked in a spreadsheet until there's budget for tools. Use CrewOS's own Outreach Agent to draft — you are customer #1 and the demo.

## Week 1 — Weaponize the founder

- **Day 1–2:** Deploy (DEPLOYMENT.md). Connect live Stripe. Record a 3-minute Loom walking through one Outreach run → approval → export. This video is your pitch, follow-up asset, and landing-page demo.
- **Day 2–3:** Build the list. Sources: your LinkedIn connections (warmest), agencies posting "hiring SDR/content" jobs (they have the budget and the pain — job boards are intent data), Product Hunt/YC directory companies, local agency directories. Target: 150 named prospects with company + human + one observed pain detail.
- **Day 3–5:** Warm-channel first. DM/email every relevant person you already know with the Loom: *"Built X, paying customers get [outcome], who should I show this to?"* Warm intros convert 10–50x cold. Then start cold: 25/day, personalized first line from their public work.

## Week 2 — 10 design partners

- Offer to your ICP: **"Design-partner deal: $199/mo (locked forever) for weekly 20-minute feedback calls. I'll set up your first agent with you."** Goal: 10 calls, 5 signed.
- Run every call the same way: their real ICP → run the Outreach/Proposal agent live → watch their face when the scored leads appear. Sell the approval queue: "nothing sends without you."
- Ship the exact thing each design partner complains about. Their wins (hours saved, replies) become case studies with numbers.

## Week 3 — Public launch

- **Tuesday 00:01 PT:** Product Hunt (prep Sunday: assets, first-comment, 5 friends on standby for honest support — never ask for fake upvotes).
- **Same day:** Hacker News "Show HN" — lead with the technical story (single-app architecture, DB-backed queue, model router, zero-key demo mode) not the marketing story.
- **All week:** answer every comment within 10 minutes (that's the whole game), then convert traffic → demo workspace → design-partner offer.
- Publish 2 SEO/listicle pages you can win ("AI proposal generator for agencies", "Upwork alternatives for prospecting") — the Content Agent drafts them.

## Week 4 — Convert & systematize

- Close the waiting list: 48-hour trial→annual offer ("2 months free, price locked").
- Start the **daily engine** (below) with a second niche vertical.
- First affiliate/referral asks: every design partner gets their referral link + 1-paragraph blurb.
- Retro: which niche closed fastest? Concentrate next month's list there. Kill channels with <2% call-book rate.

## The daily engine (persists after day 30)

| Slot | Action |
|---|---|
| 08:00 | 25 new cold touches (10 email, 10 LinkedIn, 5 custom) |
| 10:00 | 1 demo call or Loom send to warm replies |
| 13:00 | 15 follow-ups on 3-day-old touches (follow-ups create ~70% of replies) |
| 16:00 | 1 public content artifact (post/case study/build-log) |
| 17:00 | Log: touches → replies → calls → offers → closed. Review weekly only. |

## Cold email template (proven structure: observation → cost → proof → soft ask)

> **Sub: your SDR job post (2 min idea)**
>
> Hi {First} — saw {Company} is hiring an SDR. Before you pay ~$4k/mo for one seat: we built CrewOS, which runs outreach + proposals as AI employees for $499/mo total. A studio like {similar company} ran its first scored-lead sequence in 10 minutes with us.
>
> Worth a 15-min look? If it's not a fit, I'll send the 1-page teardown of our outreach playbook instead — useful either way.

Rules: <90 words, one personalized observation, no links in first touch (deliverability), 3 touches max then park. Warm up the sending domain 10+ days before volume (see DEPLOYMENT.md go-live order).

## LinkedIn script (connection → value → ask, never pitch-first)

1. Connect +: "Enjoyed your post on {topic} — the {specific point} matches what I'm seeing."
2. (after accept) "Quick q: how is your team handling prospecting + proposals this quarter — humans, tools, or a mix?"
3. (if pain) "Built a thing for exactly that. 3-min video: {link}. If it resonates, happy to run your ICP live this week."
